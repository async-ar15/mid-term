# 🧠 The Ultimate OB Exam Cheat Sheet: Memory Tricks & Hacks

Memorizing the entire Organizational Psychology syllabus doesn't have to be stressful. Use these acronyms, word associations, and one-liners to lock the concepts into your brain before the exam!

---

## 1. Classical Management Theories (The "Big Three")

**Remember the 3 T's of F.W. Taylor (Scientific Management):**
* **T**ask analysis
* **T**ime studies
* **T**echnical efficiency
* *View of worker:* An **Instrument** of production.

**Henry Ford (Fordism):**
* Think **"Mass & Machine"**. Mass production, worker reduced to a machine, zero discretion, high wage.

**Elton Mayo (Human Relations / Hawthorne):**
* Think **"Mind & Mates"**. Workers have minds (sentiments) and mates (informal groups). 
* *Hawthorne Effect:* Attention = Productivity.

---

## 2. Bureaucracy's 5 Characteristics
**Mnemonic: "Don't Hide When It's Tough" (D-H-W-I-T)**
* **D**ivision of Labor (Leads to *Trained Incapacity*)
* **H**ierarchy of Authority (Who answers to whom)
* **W**ritten Rules (Leads to *Goal Displacement*)
* **I**mpersonality (Leads to *Frustration*)
* **T**echnical Qualifications (Leads to *Peter Principle* - rising to incompetence)

*(Bonus: **Parkinson's Law** = "Work expands to fill time". Picture an old lady taking 8 hours to write one postcard).*

---

## 3. Karl Marx's Alienation
**Mnemonic: The 4 P's of Alienation**
Under capitalism, you are separated from:
1. **P**roduct (You don't own what you make)
2. **P**rocess (You don't control how you make it)
3. **P**eople (Your co-workers are rivals, not friends)
4. **P**otential (You lose your human essence/creative self)

## 4. Personality Traits

**The Big Five: O.C.E.A.N.**
* **O**penness (Creative, imaginative)
* **C**onscientiousness (Reliable, organized)
* **E**xtraversion (Sociable, outgoing)
* **A**greeableness (Cooperative, warm)
* **N**euroticism / Emotional Stability (Calm vs. anxious)

**Type A vs. Type B:**
* **Type A = A**cceleration & **A**chievement (Impatient, multitasking).
* **Type B = B**alance (Relaxed, no guilt).

## 5. Emotional Intelligence & Freud

**Emotional Intelligence: S.A.M.S.E.**
* **S**elf-awareness
* **A**nxiety control (**M**anaging emotions)
* **M**otivating oneself
* **S**ocial skills
* **E**mpathy

**Freud's Personality Trio:**
* **Id**: *"I want it NOW"* (Pleasure principle, instinct).
* **Ego**: *"Let's be realistic"* (Reality principle, the balancer).
* **Superego**: *"Is it right?"* (Morality principle, societal rules).

## 6. Stress Management

**Sources of Stress: E.O.P. ("Every Organization Panics")**
* **E**nvironmental (Economy, Tech, Politics - *Uncertainty*)
* **O**rganizational (Role overload, demanding boss, red tape)
* **P**ersonal (Family, finances)

**Types of Stressors:**
* **Challenge**: Mountains you want to climb (Workload, responsibility).
* **Hindrance**: Brick walls in your way (Red tape, role ambiguity).

---

## 7. Motivation Theories

**Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory:**
* **Hygiene = Prevent Sickness** (Extrinsic: Salary, conditions, policies). If absent, you are unhappy. If present, you are just "not unhappy."
* **Motivators = Make you Grow** (Intrinsic: Achievement, responsibility, growth). This is what actually drives you.

**McClelland's 3 Needs: A.A.P.**
* **A**chievement (Drive to excel)
* **A**ffiliation (Drive for friends)
* **P**ower (Drive to control)

**Vroom's Expectancy Theory: E.I.V.**
* **E**xpectancy: Effort $\rightarrow$ Performance (Can I do it?)
* **I**nstrumentality: Performance $\rightarrow$ Reward (Will I get something?)
* **V**alence: Reward $\rightarrow$ Personal Goal (Do I actually care about the reward?)

**Adams' Equity Theory:**
* Compare **Inputs vs. Outcomes** with others. 
* Under-rewarded = Anger. Over-rewarded = Guilt.

## 8. The Johari Window

**Mnemonic: "A.B.F.U."**
* **A**rena: I know, You know (Open it up!)
* **B**lind Spot: I don't know, You know (Ask for feedback)
* **F**açade (Hidden): I know, You don't know (Share your struggles)
* **U**nknown: I don't know, You don't know (Hidden talents)
